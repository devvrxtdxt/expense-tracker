Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information or any sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Service — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and personalise your experience. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics sold to third parties, or any purpose beyond operating Spendly.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from your profile settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with or share data with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app.
